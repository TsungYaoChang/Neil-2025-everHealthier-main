About Ever Healthier

Ever Healthier is a SMART on FHIR-based digital platform specifically designed for kidney transplant recipients and their clinical care teams.

Following kidney transplantation, patients require lifelong immunosuppressive therapy to prevent organ rejection. Even minor deviations from prescribed medication regimens can have severe consequences, including hospitalization, transplant rejection, and increased mortality rates.

Our platform enhances medication adherence, safety, and communication between patients and clinicians by providing real-time health tracking, comprehensive monitoring tools, and data-driven insights to support better post-transplant outcomes.
