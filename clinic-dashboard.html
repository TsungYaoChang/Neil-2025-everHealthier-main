Risk Level Calculation

Risk levels are calculated using a comprehensive points-based system that analyzes multiple clinical factors. The system evaluates patient data across 9 categories and assigns points based on clinical thresholds.

Risk Categories:

  • High Risk: Total score ≥ 10 points
  • Medium Risk: Total score 4-9 points
  • Normal: Total score < 4 points

Scoring Categories (9 Categories):

1. Renal Function (eGFR & Creatinine)

eGFR:

  • <30 mL/min/1.73m² (Stage 4-5): +10 points
  • <45 mL/min/1.73m² (Stage 3b): +6 points
  • <60 mL/min/1.73m² (Stage 3a): +3 points

Creatinine:

  • >2.5 mg/dL: +8 points
  • >2.0 mg/dL: +5 points
  • >1.5 mg/dL: +2 points
2. Renal Function Trends (90 days)
  • eGFR decline >10 units: +5 points
  • eGFR decline >5 units: +2 points
3. Immunosuppressant Levels (Tacrolimus)
  • Below target range: +6 points
  • Above target range: +4 points

Target ranges vary by time post-transplant

4. Inflammatory Markers

C-Reactive Protein (CRP):

  • >10 mg/L: +4 points
  • >5 mg/L: +2 points

White Blood Cell Count (WBC):

  • <3,000 cells/μL (<3.0 ×10³/μL): +3 points
  • >12,000 cells/μL (>12.0 ×10³/μL): +3 points

Normal range: 4,000-11,000 cells/μL (4.0-11.0 ×10³/μL)

5. Medication Adherence (7 days)
  • <60%: +7 points
  • <80%: +3 points
6. Blood Pressure Trends (14 days)
  • ≥160/100 mmHg (Severe): +4 points
  • ≥140/90 mmHg (Hypertension): +2 points
7. Weight Changes (14 days)
  • >3 kg change: +3 points
  • >2 kg change: +1 point
8. Proteinuria
  • >1000 mg/24h: +5 points
  • >500 mg/24h: +2 points
9. Recent Clinical Events
  • Recent rejection episode: +8 points
  • Recent infection: +4 points

Example Calculation:

eGFR 42 mL/min/1.73m² (Stage 3b) +6

Tacrolimus below target +6

Medication adherence 65% +3

Total Score: 15 points

Risk Level: HIGH RISK

Note: This comprehensive risk assessment uses evidence-based clinical thresholds to provide early detection of potential complications in kidney transplant patients. Regular monitoring and timely intervention based on these risk levels can significantly improve patient outcomes.
